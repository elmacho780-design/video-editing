## Music
This is a new theme for the "Say It Like a Local" series, in the same family as the other two:
- G major at 128 BPM, on real samples
- the same landing chord on the end card

It has a café feel of its own: swung rhythm, jazzy chords, a walking bass, vibraphone and a cajón. It is written to the conversation:
- **The first frame:** the shop door's chime.
- **The barista's questions:** the vibraphone asks with three rising notes.
- **Your answers:** the marimba answers with two falling notes, and the ✓ chime is the note that lands it home.
- **The awkward "...":** the whole band stops for a beat.
- **The recap:** the receipt prints one note a line, and the stamp lands on the last chord before the end card.

## Notes for Batu
- **Why not "I want coffee":** it's grammatical, but it sounds like a child's demand. "Can I have…, please?" is what people actually say. "Can I get…?" is also very common, especially in the US.
- **"To go" or "take away":** "To go" is American and is understood everywhere. In the UK the barista asks "Eat in or take away?" The video uses "to go" with a small "UK: take away" note. Tell me if you'd rather lead with the British one.
- **Pronunciation:** "latte" is "LAH-tay".
- **Pace when recording:** say each line straight after the last one, without pauses between them. About 15 s of speech in total fits.
- **Please check these Mongolian words on screen:** Бариста, Та, ХАДГАЛААРАЙ!
