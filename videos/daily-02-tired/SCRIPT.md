  - Level 3 **fatigued**: formal, the word doctors and reports use. It is not "more tired than exhausted", it is more formal.

## Music
This is a new theme for the "One Word, Three Levels" series, in the same family as the "Stop Saying This" theme:
- G major at 128 BPM, on the same instruments
- the same landing chord on the end card, so the whole series sounds like one brand

Its own tune starts every phrase with three rising steps. The band climbs with the levels:
- **The hook:** the glockenspiel asks a question.
- **Level 1:** the tune comes in on marimba.
- **Level 2:** drums join.
- **Level 3:** everything plays.
- **The recap:** the drums drop out.

Each level's word lands with a pop one step higher than the last (G, then B, then D). The recap replays those three notes as the steps are named. The band leaves the ✓'s beat free so it is heard.

## Notes for Batu
- **Pronunciation:**
  - Say **exhausted** with a silent h ("ig-ZAW-stid"). The card fades the h out and says so.
  - **fatigued** is two syllables ("fuh-TEEGD").
- **Pace when recording:** say each line straight after the last one, without pauses between them. About 15 s of speech in total fits.
- **The "very" tip:** "exhausted" already means "very tired", so it takes *absolutely*, *completely* or *totally*, not *very*. If you'd rather say "completely exhausted", tell me and I'll swap the tile.
- **Please check these Mongolian words:**
  - the register labels: энгийн, хүчтэй, албан ёсны
  - the meanings: ядарсан; туйлдсан, хүч барагдсан; ядарсан, сульдсан
  - the note: h-г дуудахгүй
  - the bookmark: Хадгалаарай!
