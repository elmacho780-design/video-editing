# HML olympiad Reel: storyboard and style

Sheet: `stills/board_0.jpg` (ten style frames mocked up on stills from the real footage, plus the style legend).
Footage: one 46 s take, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Edited to about 42 s. Reference study: `videos/reference-edits/REFERENCE.md`.

## Style

We use the reference Reels' grammar but HML's own look, so the edit can't be mistaken for theirs:

| | References | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Caption pill | White glass, black text, yellow keyword pill (Winner's) | Warm frosted glass with an orange inner rim, HML navy text, keyword on the **logo's orange-to-amber** pill in white |
| Type | Gilroy-style heavy geometric | **Unbounded** for keywords and numbers, **Manrope** for captions |
| Explainer scene | Pastel blob gradient | **Navy night to sky blue**, orange glow, faint HML tree line art, her face in a round badge with an orange ring |
| Signature transition | Diagonal glass-pane wipe; pill morph | **Bloom wipe**: HML tree branches draw up from the bottom, flowers pop open, and the next shot blooms through them |
| Emphasis | Yellow glowing frame | **Navy frame with an orange rim**, the HML crest and "Olympiad prep" on the corner |

### Rules
- **Graphics:** one per spoken idea, entering on the word. Entrances blur in and scale up in under 170 ms; exits blur out; swaps overlap.
- **Layout:** cards sit in the empty wall above her head; captions sit on her sweater just under her chin (y about 1230 px); nothing goes in the bottom Reels caption area or the right-hand button column.
- **Pauses:** pauses between sentences are trimmed, and each jump cut is hidden with an alternating 100 / 108% punch-in.
- **Captions:** 1 to 3 words per pill, timed to her words (aligned with Whisper large-v3). The pill stretches to fit each phrase. Questions and the offer use the two-tier caption: a small glass pill above a big tilted orange one.

## Music

- **The track:** original, bright and modern at 112 BPM: marimba, pizzicato strings, light claps and shaker, and a glockenspiel sparkle as cards land.
- **Under the voice:** about 20 dB under her voice, ducking around words.
- **Accents:**
  - a swell into each bloom wipe
  - a rising note per olympiad tile
  - a dead stop for the comic shout
- **End card:** the music comes forward.
- **Licensing:** CC0 samples only.

## Spoken words that differ from the transcript

The captions follow what she actually says. Whisper large-v3 hears these differences (for Batu to check):
- **Line 1:** "3–6 сарын англи хэлийг нь" ("хугацаанд" isn't heard).
- **Line 3:** "…USA Olympiad **гэсэн том том олимпиадууд байна**" (not "зэрэг томоохон олимпиадууд багтана").
- **Line 7:** "3 **дээр нэмэх нь 1** буюу 3 сараар нь…" (she says it out loud; the caption shows **3+1**).
