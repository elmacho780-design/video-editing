# HML olympiad Reel: storyboard and style

Sheet: `stills/board_0.jpg` (ten style frames mocked up on stills from the real footage, plus the style legend).
Footage: one 46 s take, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Edited to about 42 s. Reference study: `videos/reference-edits/REFERENCE.md`.

## Style: "HML notebook"

Changed after review so the edit can't be mistaken for the references: no frosted glass, no blur-ins, no yellow keyword pill, no glowing frame. HML makes books, so the graphics are paper, stickers, tape and stamps.

| | References | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Captions | Frosted glass pill, black text, yellow keyword pill | **Navy sticker label**, thick white border, hard orange offset shadow, alternating tilt; keyword turns orange with a **hand-drawn squiggle** |
| Two-tier caption | Small pill above a big yellow pill | Small sticker above a **paper ticket** with notched ends |
| Cards | Translucent glass widgets | **Notebook paper** (ruled lines, orange margin, navy outline, hard shadow) held on with **washi tape** |
| Motion | Blur in and out | **Slap on**: a rotation snap and a small bounce, no blur |
| Explainer scene | UI scene with a round face badge | **Graph-paper page**, her face as a taped photo sticker, olympiads as numbered stickers with **hand-drawn ticks** |
| Numbers | Typed with a caret | **Split-flap board** flipping digit by digit |
| Emphasis | Glowing coloured frame | **Scrolling ribbon band** and an **HML rubber stamp** |
| Brand tag | Pill top left | **Bookmark ribbon** hanging from the top edge |
| Transition | Glass-pane wipe, pill morph | **Bloom wipe** from the HML logo tree |
| Palette | Yellow and black; pastel blobs; electric blue | HML navy `#14295A`, HML orange `#FF6A2B`, sky blue `#4EA3FF`, paper `#F8F2E4` |
| Type | Gilroy-style | Unbounded (keywords, numbers) and Manrope (captions) |

### Rules
- **Graphics:** one per spoken idea, slapped on as the word is said.
- **Layout:** cards go in the empty wall above her head; captions go on her sweater (y about 1230 px); nothing goes in the Reels caption area or the right-hand button column.
- **Pauses:** pauses are trimmed, and each jump cut is hidden with an alternating 100 / 108% punch-in.
- **Captions:** 1 to 3 words per sticker, timed to her words (aligned with Whisper large-v3).

## Music

- **The track:** original, bright and modern at 112 BPM: marimba, pizzicato strings, light claps and shaker, and a glockenspiel sparkle as cards land.
- **Under the voice:** about 20 dB under her voice, ducking around words.
- **Accents:**
  - a swell into each bloom wipe
  - a rising note per olympiad tile
  - a dead stop for the comic shout
- **End card:** the music comes forward.
- **Licensing:** CC0 samples only.
