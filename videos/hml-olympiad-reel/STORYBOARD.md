# HML olympiad Reel: storyboard

Footage: one 46 s take, 1080x1920, 30 fps. The presenter holds a mic against a plain wall. The first 1.3 s is camera set-up and is cut.
Reference: `videos/reference-edits/REFERENCE.md` (the three Reels studied frame by frame). We borrow the grammar, not the look.

## Our look (different from the references on purpose)

| | References | Ours |

| Accent | Winner's yellow pill with black text; edita's electric-blue italics | **HML orange-to-amber gradient** pill (from the logo's brain-tree) with white text |

| Font | Gilroy-style heavy geometric | **Unbounded** (wide display, Cyrillic) for keywords, **Manrope** ExtraBold for caption pills |
| UI-scene background | Pastel yellow, blue and peach blobs | **Navy night sky to sky-blue** gradient with soft orange glow and faint brain-tree line art |
| Signature transition | Diagonal glass-pane wipe; pill morphs into the end card | **Bloom wipe**: orange branches of the HML tree draw across the screen, flowers pop open, and the next scene is revealed through them |
| Emphasis frame | Yellow glowing border | **Navy frame with an orange rim and the HML crest** on its corner |

Motion grammar kept from the references: one graphic per spoken idea, blur-in and blur-out entrances under 170 ms, pills that stretch to fit each phrase, overlapping swaps, punch-ins on key words, a clean loop.

## Edit

- Trim the pauses between sentences (about 0.3 to 0.6 s each), so 46 s becomes about **40 s**.
- Hide each jump cut with an alternating punch-in (100% and 112%), the way the references do.
- Captions follow the speech word by word, from Whisper word timings corrected against the transcript.

## Music

An original track, written to the edit like the last one, but modern and bright to suit a talking-head ad rather than a fairy tale:
- **Instruments:** marimba and pizzicato strings, a glockenspiel sparkle on each card, a soft hand-clap and shaker groove at about 112 BPM, and warm string pads.
- **Under the voice:** the music sits about 20 dB down and ducks around every word, as in the references, so the voice always leads.
- **Accents:**
  - a rising swell into each bloom wipe
  - a small rising run as the four olympiad tiles land, one note per tile
  - a sparkle on the "+1 сар ҮНЭГҮЙ" tile
- **End card:** the music comes forward when the voice stops, with a final chord as the loop point.
- **Licensing:** CC0 samples only, so it's safe for an ad.

## Sound effects (light, as in the references)

- Soft glass "tick" when a card lands.
- Key clicks while the numbers type.
- A paper "whoosh" on each bloom wipe.
- No effect on every caption; the rhythm comes from the cuts.
