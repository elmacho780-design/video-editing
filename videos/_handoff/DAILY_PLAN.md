# HML Daily English: the plan for each day

The plan for each of the 30 days, as shared with Batu on 2026-11-06. **The live, editable version is Batu's doc "HML Daily English: 30-day plan"** (https://claude.ai/artifact/N9KHLgTDqg9ucmtcAusxoT). If he has edited it, his doc wins: read it before starting a day.

The series rules are in `DAILY_ENGLISH_SERIES.md`. Animated days run 15 to 20 s; filmed days run 30 to 60 s. All the Mongolian is written per day in that day's `SCRIPT.md` and flagged for Batu.

## Filming days

Batch-film Days 5, 7, 13, 14, 21, 27, 28 and 30 (plus 20 if filmed) in one or two sessions. The set-up for every take:
- the phone vertical on a tripod at eye level, 30 fps
- light in front, in a quiet room, with the mic close
- his face in the upper middle, with the bottom fifth and the right edge clear
- two seconds of silence around each take, and three tries at each hook

He uploads the raw files to Google Drive with "Anyone with the link".
