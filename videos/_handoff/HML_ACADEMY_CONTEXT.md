## 2. What we teach

### The three classes on offer
1. **ABC**: learning the letters (young beginners).
2. **Level Up / Түвшин ахиулах**: children who have fallen behind or want to improve.
3. **Olympiad / Олимпиадын бэлтгэл**: preparing to compete against international students.

### Focus areas
- **English olympiads:** HIPPO, SOF (International English Olympiad, IEO), USA English Olympiad (incl. USA Eagles).
- **Exams:** ЭЕШ (Mongolian general entrance exam), IELTS, university entrance, PSAT (Reading and Writing only).
- **General English:** grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing from beginner upward.

### Key facts
- HML is a **registered SOF exam centre**. Its students sat the SOF olympiad last year.
- HIPPO: we coach students up to National and World/London Finals level (e.g. an 11th-grade C1 silver medallist, a Grade 6 C1 student in category S1/S2, another in S4).
- Core teaching belief (used in ads): most centres teach grammar and vocabulary in isolation; olympiad success, especially passing HIPPO's second stage, needs students to understand context and real use.

### Current courses (autumn 2026)
- **SOF IEO prep class**, grades 5 to 9, two tracks (A: grades 5 to 7, B: grades 8 to 9). 3 x 90 min per week, 12 Oct to 15 Nov 2026. Exam on **17 Nov 2026**. 10 Nov is a holiday (Chinggis Khaan's Birthday).
- **SOF intensive**: 1 month, 14 lessons, Middle School and High School tracks. Numbering starts at Lesson 2 (Lesson 1, present tense and nouns, already taught).
- **University entrance 12-lesson series** (Lesson 1: verb patterns, used to / be used to / get used to).
- **Continental B2 vocabulary series** (11 lessons, Lesson 1: Personality and Character).
- **Placement test**: being replaced with a harder 40-minute version (taken mostly by grades 5 to 7; also spots olympiad candidates).
- Individual students: IELTS (vocabulary focus), PSAT, HIPPO finalists.

## 5. House rules for every document

**Language and style**
- **British English** always.
- **No em-dashes anywhere.** Use commas, colons, full stops or brackets.
- Arial / Helvetica. For Mongolian Cyrillic in PDFs, use Liberation Sans (check Ө ө Ү ү Ё ё render); DejaVu Sans for ✓ ✗ ☐.
- Mongolian translations Claude writes should be flagged for Batu to check.

**Layout**
- A4, print-ready.
- Colours: navy / soft-blue headers, light grey fills, **maroon trap boxes**.
- Footer crediting Battumur.D (see exceptions below).
- No sparse or half-empty pages.
- Kids' books use a brighter, friendlier palette instead of navy/maroon.
- When asked to save ink, interiors go greyscale.

**Answer keys**
- **Always a separate file.** Never inside the student document.
- Usual output: student booklet + separate teacher guide / answer key.

**Branding exceptions**
- **Placement tests:** HML logo only. No "Curated by Battumur.D", no personal name, no olympiad wording.
- **SOF prep class materials:** branded just **"HML"** (not "HML Academy"), no personal credit line.
- Otherwise default to HML Academy + "Curated by Battumur.D".

**Teaching approach**
- Materials must be **teachable lessons**, not self-study worksheets: substantial explanations for the teacher to present, plus a high volume of exercises.
- Teach real language (grammar systems, vocabulary), not exam tricks or step-by-step test strategy.
- Explanations plain enough to follow at a glance. One idea per sentence. Show examples before naming the term.
- Common lesson structure: rule box, worked example, trap warning (maroon), practice. 90-minute lessons are standard.
- Favourite exercise types: gap-fill, word completion, word formation, error correction, translation, crosswords, unscrambles, word search, matching, timed mock rounds.
- Keep content age-appropriate (check distractor words too).

**Formats and tooling**
- PDFs built with Python + ReportLab (pypdf / pikepdf for assembly). Word files as .docx.
- Always open and render-check the output. A past .docx came out as one 215-page line, so verify Word files actually display properly.
